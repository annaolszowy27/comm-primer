Theory becomes useful when it changes practice. Across these schools, several durable habits emerge:

1. Remember how important culture is to understand the messages you’re receiving. Context, culture, and background can change the meaning of a message drastically. Whoever it is from, their position and knowledge all effect what they are trying to ask or say. 
2. Be cautious about assumptions. Not everyone will understand a message the same way, which means you should critically think before responding or spreading it. Symbols all don’t have the same meaning. 
3. Keep informed about AI and the developments in technology around the world. This modern era can bring a lot of fast changing updates that if you get behind on, can be a detriment professionally and personally. 
4. Communication is more complex than some theories make it out to be. It will not always be straightforward; anything can happen when communicating with others. Know there might be noise of misinformation included. 
5. Be aware of who has the power to control communication and messages being sent out to the public. 

Understanding these points can help you be a clear, effective communicator in school, personal situations, and professionally.

The transmission view is contributed to by the Shannon-Weaver model of communication. It includes the steps of sender, encoder, channel, decoder, and receiver. However, within this process the message can also experience noise. This affects if the message was sent successfully or not.

In modern connections, there are many opportunities for the channel to experience noise such as losing Internet, static, or misinterpretations. This applies to AI usage since while there are technical issues, misinformation can skew the result as well. AI is not 100% verified and can give false information in response to a question. 

An argument that has been made about this model is that the steps simplify communication too much. There are numerous other factors that can go into forms of communication such as phrases, tones, emotions, and physical gestures that need to be solved. AI cannot necessarily interpret all these correctly and would possibly work better following the Shannon-Weaver model, going through the steps. 

This model can be used when explaining how messages are transmitted through a channel, when a message is disrupted, or break down what a coworker or classmate is trying to say.

Semiotics shifts attention from delivery to signification. Communication is not complete when a message arrives; it is complete when signs are interpreted in cultural context.

This perspective helps explain why identical words, images, or prompts can produce different reactions across communities. A sign carries histories, assumptions, and values.

In AI-mediated communication, semiotics helps readers identify when outputs appear clear but remain culturally unstable. Meaning is negotiated, not simply transmitted.

Use this lens to study symbols, narratives, and recurring patterns that shape interpretation over time.
